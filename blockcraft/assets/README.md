# Assets

Dinosaurs are extinct, so there's no such thing as a "real" T-Rex or velociraptor recording —
the two dinosaur sounds below are genuine animal recordings commonly used as stand-ins for
them, matching the fact that paleontology places dinosaurs in the crocodilian/avian family
tree rather than anything that could plausibly roar like a movie monster.

## lion-roar.ogg

A recording of a lion roaring in captivity (zoo, Tamil Nadu).

- Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lion_raring-sound1TamilNadu178.ogg)
- Uploaded by: தகவலுழவன் (Info-farmer)
- License: released into the **public domain** by the uploader — no attribution required
- Used in-game trimmed to the first ~2.2 seconds, with a short fade-out

## trex-roar.ogg

An American alligator bellowing — a real crocodilian roar standing in for the T-Rex, one of
its closest living relatives among extant animals.

- Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:27alligator2bellow.ogg), originally from the [U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service sound library](https://www.fws.gov/video/sound.htm)
- License: **public domain** (U.S. federal government work) — no attribution required
- Used in-game trimmed to ~3 seconds, with a short fade-out

## raptor-screech.mp3

A red-tailed hawk call — fitting the velociraptor, since paleontology places raptors on the
bird lineage of dinosaurs rather than the reptilian one.

- Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Buteo_jamaicensis_-_Red-tailed_Hawk_XC71575.mp3), recorded by Jonathon Jongsma at Dordt College Prairie, Sioux, Iowa, via [xeno-canto.org](https://xeno-canto.org)
- License: **CC BY-SA 3.0** — attribution required (given here); any redistribution of this specific file must carry the same license
- Used in-game at its original ~2.2 second length

Everything else in the game (textures, all other sound effects) is generated procedurally at
runtime — these three are the only real asset files. Each falls back to a synthesized sound
automatically if it fails to load, so the game still works without them.
