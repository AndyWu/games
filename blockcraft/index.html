Break blocks to gather materials, then place your Crafting Table and press E next to it to craft — including windows and doors, which you can right-click to open or close. Cows and sheep are harmless — dogs, giraffes, lions and elephants will fight back if you attack them, and lions and elephants will attack on sight if you get too close.
