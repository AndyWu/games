Click anywhere to play

Break blocks to gather materials, then place your Crafting Table and press E next to it to craft — including windows and doors, which you can right-click to open or close. Cows and sheep are harmless — dogs, giraffes, lions and elephants will fight back if you attack them, lions and elephants will attack on sight if you get too close, and a T-Rex and a pack of velociraptors actively hunt anything nearby, animal or player. Progress is saved automatically in this browser.
